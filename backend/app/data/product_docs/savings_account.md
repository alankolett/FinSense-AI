# SBI Savings Account Products

We offer several types of savings accounts to cater to different segments.

## 1. SBI Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) Account
- **Eligibility**: Any individual with valid KYC documents.
- **Minimum Balance**: Nil.
- **Maximum Balance / Transactions**: No upper limit on balance, but limited to 4 free cash withdrawals per month.
- **Interest Rate**: 2.70% per annum.
- **Key Features**: Free ATM-cum-Debit Card, no annual maintenance charges.

## 2. SBI Savings Plus Account
- **Description**: Linked to Multi Option Deposit (MOD) Scheme. Any surplus amount above Rs. 25,000 is automatically transferred to Fixed Deposits in multiples of Rs. 1,000.
- **Minimum Balance Requirement**: Rs. 25,000.
- **Interest Rate**: Same as regular savings account for balance in account; higher FD rates for MOD portion.
- **Key Features**: High liquidity with higher returns.

## 3. SBI Yuva Savings Account
- **Target Audience**: Youth aged 18 to 30 years.
- **Minimum Balance**: Rs. 500.
- **Features**: Free mobile banking, internet banking, and a customized contactless debit card.
