# SBI Credit Card Products

SBI Card offers a range of cards tailored to lifestyle, shopping, travel, and reward preferences.

## 1. SBI Card ELITE
- **Annual Fee**: Rs. 4,999 + taxes.
- **Welcome Gift**: E-gift voucher worth Rs. 5,000.
- **Key Features**: 
  - 5x Reward Points on Dining, Departmental Stores, and Grocery spend.
  - Free movie tickets worth Rs. 6,000 every year.
  - Complimentary domestic and international airport lounge visits.
- **Best For**: Premium users, luxury shoppers, and frequent flyers.

## 2. SimplySAVE SBI Card
- **Annual Fee**: Rs. 499 + taxes (waived on annual spends of Rs. 1,00,000 or more).
- **Key Features**:
  - 10 Reward Points per Rs. 150 spent on Dining, Movies, Departmental Stores, and Grocery.
  - 1 Reward Point per Rs. 150 on other spends.
  - 1% Fuel Surcharge waiver across all petrol pumps in India.
- **Best For**: Everyday shopping and value-conscious spenders.

## 3. SBI Card PRIME
- **Annual Fee**: Rs. 2,999 + taxes.
- **Key Features**:
  - 10 Reward Points per Rs. 100 spent on Dining, Groceries, Departmental Stores, and Dining.
  - 20 Reward Points per Rs. 100 spent on birthday.
  - Trident Privilege Red Tier membership and Club Vistara Silver membership.
- **Best For**: Lifestyle, dining, and travel rewards.
